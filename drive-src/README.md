# Splash Racer & Mad Taxi Masti — by BajrangVerse Studios

Two driving games built on one shared engine (three.js in an Android WebView, like Rocky's Pirate Army).

**Splash Racer** (`src/splash`, app `../splashapp`, `com.bajrangverse.splashracer`): a kart race where the road ends
in a river — the karts fly off a ramp, splash down as speedboats, and turn back into karts on the far bank. Five funny
rivals with live faces and speech bubbles (Speedy Sonu, Aunty Rocket, Turbo Tinku, Dizzy Dolly, Professor Pappu), a boss
race every 5th race (Mr. Moneybags), cows on the road, mud, oil, fruit carts, big-air ramps with flips, buoys, a podium,
and a garage of karts, boats and helmet colours. Drag to steer, NITRO button, swipe up to hop (and flip in the air).

**Mad Taxi Masti** (`src/taxi`, app `../taxiapp`, `com.bajrangverse.madtaxi`): drive a city taxi (India drives on the
left; the other side has oncoming traffic). Each ride is a crazy passenger with a goal of their own: Dulha Raju (late
for his wedding: timer), Dadi Ji (12 eggs: drive smooth), Sleepy Sharma Uncle (no horn, no bumps, 3 wake-ups and he
walks), Insta Pinky (stunts for likes), Detective Aunty (chase the thief's car), Cricket Bunty (hit the 4 and 6 signs),
Boss Khanna (quiet during calls) and Little Chintu (balloons and honking). A mood meter, a live back-seat camera of the
passenger (real 3D faces and expressions), cows that move only when you honk, speed breakers, potholes, dogs, ramps,
tips and funny reviews, a garage of taxis and horns. Drag to steer, hold BRAKE, tap HORN, TURBO.

## Build
    # node_modules: npm install (three, esbuild, javascript-obfuscator, playwright, @fontsource/fredoka)
    PROTECT=1 node build.mjs            # both games → www-splash/, www-taxi/ and the apps' src/main/assets (obfuscated)
    PROTECT=1 node build.mjs taxi       # one game
    cd .. && gradle :splashapp:assembleRelease :splashapp:bundleRelease :taxiapp:assembleRelease :taxiapp:bundleRelease

The apps' `src/main/assets` folders are generated by `build.mjs` (not kept in git).

Characters (only when they change): `node tools/people3.mjs` writes the cast (`tools/people3.json`), then with the CC0
Quaternius packs (Universal Base Characters, Modular Character Outfits - Fantasy, Universal Animation Library 1 + 2,
Ultimate Animated Character Pack hats) served at http://localhost:8765/:
`OUT=assets/people-splash.bin node tools/bake3.mjs racerM,racerF` and
`OUT=assets/people-taxi.bin node tools/bake3.mjs groom,granny,uncle,influencer,detective,fan,boss,kid,pedM,pedF`.
Faces get eyebrows and three mouth shapes (smile / open / frown) so the game can show happy, laughing, angry, scared,
sad and calm expressions; accessories (turban, garland, glasses, bindi, cap, helmet, moustache) are added by the baker.
Vehicles, boats and the city (Kenney, CC0) and the animals (Quaternius, CC0): `node tools/kit3.mjs` → `assets/kit.glb`,
`cow.glb`, `pug.glb` (Kenney packs served at http://localhost:8766/flat/).

## Code
- `src/common/engine.js` renderer, lights, particles, pop-up texts, banners, the game loop (adaptive sharpness),
  anti-rehosting guard; `gfx.js` road bend shader, per-vertex lighting (fast on phones), merging of scenery
- `src/common/track.js` the road (lanes, pavements, rivers with ramps) and the themed scenery; `vehicles.js` cars/boats
- `src/common/people.js` animated people with facial expressions (instanced crowds + heroes); `portrait.js` the
  cartoon faces in the HUD
- `src/common/audio.js`, `sfx.js` (horns, engine hum, voices, moo/woof), `music.js`; `ui.js` saves, settings, gift, ads
- `src/splash/main.js`, `src/taxi/main.js` the two games; `html/` their pages (`base.css` shared)
