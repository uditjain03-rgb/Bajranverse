# Splash Racer & Mad Taxi Masti — by BajrangVerse Studios

Two driving games built on one shared engine (three.js in an Android WebView, like Rocky's Pirate Army).

**Splash Racer** (`src/splash`, app `../splashapp`, `com.bajrangverse.splashracer`): a speedboat race across eight
water worlds (`src/common/course.js`): Tropical Lagoon, Jungle River (waterfalls), Red Canyon Rapids, Frozen Fjord,
Ghats at Sunset, Neon Harbour (night, fireworks), Stormy Sea (lightning, pirate cannon) and Volcano Island (falling lava).
Real moving waves (a water shader; the boats float on the same waves on the CPU), foam wakes, waterfalls that drop the
race to a lower level, wave ramps with tricks (swipe up = backflip, left/right = barrel roll), whirlpools, dolphins,
crocodiles, icebergs with penguins, ferries, and mystery bubbles: water balloon, duck mines, bubble shield, rocket, octopus
ink, mega wave. Five funny rivals who use items too, a boss every 4th race, a floating podium, a boatyard (5 boats,
colours). Races are 3–4.6 km. Ocean-blue look; music: Fluffing a Duck, Happy Happy Game Show, Wallpaper, Sneaky Snitch.
**v4 (Splash Rescue rework):** the Hero Rescue Boat (default): an open passenger well with padded benches, so the rescued
animals sit inside it (facing you, in orange life jackets), a fender tube in your boat colour, a T-top with flashing
blue/red beacons. 3 ANIMAL HOME docks per rescue and bigger boats (6 seats on the hero boat, +1 on the others).
Signs no longer grow with distance: they fade in from about 70 m, keep their real size, and stand on a pole over what
they mark; a pointer at the top shows the nearest animal (metres, left/right) or the next dock when the boat is full.
All living things and obstacles are smooth, shaded models (`src/common/sea.js`): animals with glossy eyes and fur
gradients, dolphins, whale, shark, crocodiles, knobbly rocks, bark logs, barrels, icebergs, a ghats river boat with
passengers, a harbour ferry with lit windows. New fun: a golden ⭐ star animal per rescue, rescue streaks, geysers that
blast you up, treasure chests, slalom hoops, seagulls and jumping fish. Worlds: rounded hills instead of cone mountains,
soft clouds, a sunset sun, wet-sand shorelines, layered canyon cliffs, smooth ice cliffs, coconut palms, lily pads and
reeds, shore rocks.

**Mad Taxi Masti** (`src/taxi`, app `../taxiapp`, `com.bajrangverse.madtaxi`): drive a real kaali-peeli taxi through eight
Indian places (`src/common/street.js`), each matched to its passenger: Shaadi Street at night (groom late for his
wedding), Old Bazaar (Dadi Ji's 12 eggs), Mountain Ghat Road (sleeping uncle), Marine Drive by the sea (influencer),
Monsoon City (detective chase), Stadium Road (cricket fan), Cyber City (boss on calls), Holi Festival (kid). Smash fruit
carts, pots and colour carts for MASTI combos, ramps with 360° spins, power-ups (magnet, mega horn, bumper shield), a
police chase when you smash too much, baraat processions and fans crossing, puddles to splash, sea waves over the road,
mid-ride stop requests for tips, a live 3D back-seat camera with the passenger's real expressions. All vehicles are made
in code with real proportions and glossy paint (`src/common/cars.js`): Padmini taxi, sedans, hatchbacks, SUVs,
auto-rickshaws, the red double-decker bus, the painted Horn-OK-Please truck, the police Gypsy, and the boats.

## Build
    # node_modules: npm install (three, esbuild, javascript-obfuscator, playwright, @fontsource/fredoka)
    PROTECT=1 node build.mjs            # both games → www-splash/, www-taxi/ and the apps' src/main/assets (obfuscated)
    PROTECT=1 node build.mjs taxi       # one game
    cd .. && gradle :splashapp:assembleRelease :splashapp:bundleRelease :taxiapp:assembleRelease :taxiapp:bundleRelease

The apps' `src/main/assets` folders are generated by `build.mjs` (not kept in git).

Characters (only when they change): `node tools/people3.mjs` writes the cast (`tools/people3.json`), then with the CC0
Quaternius packs (Universal Base Characters, Modular Character Outfits - Fantasy, Universal Animation Library 1 + 2,
Ultimate Animated Character Pack hats) served at http://localhost:8765/:
`OUT=assets/people-splash.bin node tools/bake3.mjs racerM,racerF` and
`OUT=assets/people-taxi.bin node tools/bake3.mjs groom,granny,uncle,influencer,detective,fan,boss,kid,pedM,pedF`.
Faces get eyebrows and three mouth shapes (smile / open / frown) so the game can show happy, laughing, angry, scared,
sad and calm expressions; accessories (turban, garland, glasses, bindi, cap, helmet, moustache) are added by the baker.
Vehicles, boats and the city (Kenney, CC0) and the animals (Quaternius, CC0): `node tools/kit3.mjs` → `assets/kit.glb`,
`cow.glb`, `pug.glb` (Kenney packs served at http://localhost:8766/flat/).

## Code
- `src/common/engine.js` renderer, lights, particles, pop-up texts, banners, the game loop (adaptive sharpness),
  anti-rehosting guard; `gfx.js` road bend shader, per-vertex lighting (fast on phones), merging of scenery
- `src/common/sea.js` Splash Racer's animals, sea creatures, obstacles, ferries, fun things and bank nature (smooth
  merged models with vertex colours); `course.js` its water courses
- `src/common/track.js` the road (lanes, pavements, rivers with ramps) and the themed scenery; `vehicles.js` cars/boats
- `src/common/people.js` animated people with facial expressions (instanced crowds + heroes); `portrait.js` the
  cartoon faces in the HUD
- `src/common/audio.js`, `sfx.js` (horns, engine hum, voices, moo/woof), `music.js`; `ui.js` saves, settings, gift, ads
- `src/splash/main.js`, `src/taxi/main.js` the two games; `html/` their pages (`base.css` shared)
