ABOUT US

Monster Mayhem Run is a cheerful endless runner for the whole family. Run through eleven bright worlds, help with chores along the way, collect fruits and ice creams, ride trucks, tanks and rockets, and outrun Agniraaj the Fire Monster!

🦸 THE HEROES

Ayaansh — fearless and fast. Aadhya — quick, clever and never misses a fruit. Green Monster — fluffy and giggly. Bossy — the big purple boss with a heart of gold. Bossina — sweet, pink and super tidy.

🌍 WHAT'S INSIDE

23 stages in 11 worlds, an endless mode, daily missions, a toy album, skill badges, costumes and a wardrobe for every hero, and a Fire Monster finale in every world.

🏢 BAJRANGVERSE STUDIOS

We make happy, colourful games that kids and grown-ups can play together. Monster Mayhem Run has no ads. Optional items can be bought through Google Play, and everything can also be earned by playing.

🔒 PRIVACY

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your progress is saved only on your device. Purchases are handled by Google Play; we never see your payment details. The full privacy policy is linked on our Google Play page.
